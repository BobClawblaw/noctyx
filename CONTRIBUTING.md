# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Two things before your first pull request:

1. **Contributor License Agreement.** Noctyx is licensed commercially as well as under the Business
   Source License, so every contribution needs a signed CLA: you keep your copyright and grant the
   Licensor the right to distribute your contribution under Noctyx's licenses. A bot asks you to sign
   on your first PR.
2. **No private information.** No real host names, addresses, user names or paths from your own
   systems in code, tests, docs or commit messages — use `head`, `gpu-01`, `nas` and 192.0.2.0/24.
   `python3 tools/privacy_check.py` runs in CI and locally.

Run the tests with `python3 -m pytest`.
